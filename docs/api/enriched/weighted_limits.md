# Weighted Limits

A weighted limit describes compatible ways of mapping into a diagram of objects; its
weight specifies the contributions required from the diagram [@kelly1982basic]. The routines below compute the
weighted limit and colimit of a family of parallel morphisms over a complete
lattice, as the pointwise meet of residua and join of tensors, which satisfy the
[universal property](../../guides/enriched.md#weighted-limits).
